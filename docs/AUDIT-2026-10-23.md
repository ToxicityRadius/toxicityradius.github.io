## Overall design assessment

The painted artwork, paper/green palette, Fraunces/Outfit typography and physical box/desk give the portfolio a consistent identity. Native routes, visible evidence limitations, clear contact actions, named dialogs and focus styling provide a useful foundation. Retain these choices.

The next pass should improve reliability and reading speed. Keep the approved headline and section order. A more explicit role descriptor near the hero, such as “Web applications and developer tools,” would tell employers and clients what Dexter builds before they scroll. This is an optional copy suggestion, not a factual claim about availability or experience.

Recommended order: loading interruption and shared hero height → cold-load image optimization and missing verification → card timing and case identity → archive URL state and placeholder copy → documentation/build cleanup. No new framework, backend or artwork is needed.
